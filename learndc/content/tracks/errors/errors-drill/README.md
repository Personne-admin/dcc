# Error drills

Seven short activities on what the chapter has covered: follow a chain of
`?` by hand, trace which call returns, find an ignored error and a wrong
variant, read a compiler error, and recall which type fits which job.

Each activity gives you feedback right away. A wrong answer shows what to
look at again; the full explanation appears once you get it right or after
your second try. Questions you miss come back in your review.
