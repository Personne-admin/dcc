# Modules and projects drills

Six short activities on the last lessons: read the diagnostic of a private
function, choose the file path of a module, put a manifest in order, sort
visibility rules, predict a program that mixes qualified names with `using`
and recall one keyword.

Each activity gives you feedback right away. A wrong answer shows what to
look at again; the full explanation appears once you get it right or after
your second try. Questions you miss come back in your review.
