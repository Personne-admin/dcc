# Ownership drills

Seven short activities on the rules of the last lessons: predict what runs
when, trace a function by hand, find a misplaced `defer`, read a scope
error, put code in order and recall the rules of ownership.

Each activity gives you feedback right away. A wrong answer shows what to
look at again; the full explanation appears once you get it right or after
your second try. Questions you miss come back in your review.
