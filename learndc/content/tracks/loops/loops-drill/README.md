# Loops drills

Seven short activities on loops: predict a while loop, count passes with
`continue`, find an off-by-one bug, put a loop in order, count passes of
ranges, explain a loop that never ends and recall a keyword.

Each activity gives you feedback right away. A wrong answer shows what to
look at again; the full explanation appears once you get it right or after
your second try. Questions you miss come back in your review.
