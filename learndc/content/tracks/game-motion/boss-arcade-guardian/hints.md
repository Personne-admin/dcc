## Hint 1

Work stage by stage, and run the game early. `move_paddle`, `step_ball` and `draw` are the quickest ways to see something move.

## Hint 2

Almost every function is clamping, mirroring or comparing: the paddle clamp is the player's version of the wall mirror; the paddle hit is the box-collision lesson with one axis; scoring is two comparisons on the ball's x; the AI is a comparison of two centers. Each stage has three hints of its own.

## Hint 3

See the three hints of each stage; the last ones are nearly the code.
