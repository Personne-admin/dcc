# Decisions drills

Six short activities on conditions: find a boundary bug, follow an `else if`
chain that tests in the wrong order, predict combined conditions, pick the
equivalent condition, read an if-expression error and recall an operator.

Each activity gives you feedback right away. A wrong answer shows what to
look at again; the full explanation appears once you get it right or after
your second try. Questions you miss come back in your review.
